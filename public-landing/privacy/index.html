PulseWatch pilot enquiry privacy notice

How INMAR d.o.o. processes contact details submitted through the PulseWatch managed-pilot form. Last updated 21 August 2026.

Controller: INMAR d.o.o., Gardinovec 24, 40319 Belica, Croatia. Use the INMAR contact form for privacy, access or deletion requests.

What we collect

The managed-pilot enquiry form stores the work email address you submit and the submission time. The form does not intentionally store your IP address.

Why we use it

We use the address only to review whether the proposed PulseWatch pilot is a fit, send the requested pilot information and answer related pre-contract questions. We do not sell submitted contact data.

Retention

Unconverted pilot enquiries are automatically removed from the lead file after 90 days. If you become a client, information needed for the service, contract, accounting or legal obligations is handled under the relevant client documentation and retention rules.

Service providers and access

The lead file is stored in restricted server-side storage and is not intended to be publicly accessible. Access is limited to people and service providers needed to operate the site, hosting and enquiry process. We require a separate decision and notice before adding optional notification or marketing processors.

Your choices and rights

You may ask whether we hold your enquiry, request a copy or correction, object to processing, or request deletion where applicable. Submit the request through the INMAR contact form linked above. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority.

No marketing subscription

Submitting the pilot form is not consent to an unrelated marketing newsletter. Any future marketing subscription must use a separate, explicit choice.
